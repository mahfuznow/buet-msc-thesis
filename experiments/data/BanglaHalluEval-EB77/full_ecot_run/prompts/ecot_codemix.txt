{context}

Decompose the CANDIDATE ANSWER into atomic factual claims. For each claim:

Output a SINGLE JSON object exactly matching:

  "verdict": "yes" or "no"
}}

  verdict = "yes" ONLY if either:
    (a) at least one claim is "contradicted", OR
    (b) MORE THAN HALF the claims are "missing".

  must not flip the verdict.
